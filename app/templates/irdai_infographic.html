Corporate Governance & Licensing

Definitions and rules from IRDAI Registration (2000) & Bancassurance (2012) Regulations

Bancassurance (2012)

MCEV & Amortization

Market Consistent Embedded Value (MCEV) is calculated per Institute of Actuaries methods. The Period of Amortization allows insurers to amortize the difference between MCEV and the purchase value of equity.

Registration (2000)

Indian Promoters

An "Indian Promoter" must be a non-subsidiary company, a core investment company (CIC), a cooperative society, or an LLP, ensuring strong domestic capitalization as per the 8th Amendment.

Policyholder Rights & Consumer Rules

Section 1

Free-Look Period

Policyholders have a 15 to 30-day "free-look" window after receiving the policy document to review terms and cancel for a refund if unsatisfied.

Section 2

Claim Settlement Timeline

Insurers must settle or reject a claim within 30 days of receiving all required documents. Delays attract penal interest.

Section 3

Standardized Exclusions

IRDAI standardized exclusions. Insurers cannot deny claims for telemedicine, modern treatments (e.g., robotic surgery), or mental illnesses.

Section 4

Grievance Redressal

The Bima Bharosa portal allows online complaints. Insurers must acknowledge within 3 days and resolve within 15 days.

2026 Regulatory Outlook & Ind AS Implementation

Key updates extracted from IRDAI Ind AS Circular and Global 2026 Outlook

Focus Area 1

AI & Data Governance

Regulators are prioritizing the formalization of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and data governance frameworks. This mitigates bias in automated underwriting and claims processing.

Focus Area 2

Climate Resilience

Increased regulatory emphasis on catastrophe preparedness. Insurers must update solvency requirements to close coverage gaps for climate-related risks.

Crisis Management & Standardization

Covid-19 Response

Essential Service & Mandates

During the pandemic, insurance was deemed an "essential service". IRDAI mandated that Covid-19 must be covered under all indemnity-based health insurance and set a strict 2-hour time limit to authorize cashless claims.

Standard Products

Aarogya Sanjeevani

To address the uninsured, IRDAI designed "Aarogya Sanjeevani" (a standard health policy) and specific products like "Corona Kavach" and "Corona Rakshak" to offer straightforward protection against health crises.
